Know Before You Mod

Modification Rules in India

As per Motor Vehicles Act 1988 & Central Motor Vehicles Rules

✅ Legal Modifications
Permitted under Indian Motor Vehicles Act with proper documentation
  • ✅Changing tyre size within ±3% of original diameter (requires RTO re-registration)
  • ✅Alloy wheels of same size — no RTO approval needed for same-size upgrades
  • ✅LED bulb replacements for headlamps and interior (colour must remain white/amber per rule)
  • ✅Seat covers — fabric, leather, synthetic allowed. Colour changes are fine
  • ✅Cabin audio system upgrades — sub-woofers, amplifiers, screen replacements
  • ✅Roof rack and cargo carriers with OEM or certified mounts
  • ✅Bull bars and front guards on SUVs (must not obstruct number plate)
  • ✅Window tinting — up to 70% VLT for front, 50% for rear as per SC ruling
  • ✅Suspension lift up to 50mm with proof of structural certification
  • ✅Adding snorkel / air-intake relocation on 4x4 vehicles
  • ✅CNG / LPG kit conversion (must be ARAI certified and registered with RTO)
  • ✅Disability adaptations — hand controls, wheelchair ramps (encouraged by law)
🚫 Illegal Modifications
Banned under Section 52 of Motor Vehicles Act — risk of fine, seizure, or prosecution
  • 🚫HID or xenon bulbs in halogen housings — causes dangerous glare, ₹5,000+ fine
  • 🚫Blue, red or green lights visible from front or rear — reserved for emergency vehicles
  • 🚫Pressure horns, multi-tone horns above 112 dB — noise pollution violation
  • 🚫Altering engine displacement or swapping engine without ARAI certification
  • 🚫Chassis modification or cutting/welding the body frame
  • 🚫Changing vehicle colour without RC update (RC must show same colour)
  • 🚫Dark window films below legal VLT limits — risk of challan + film removal
  • 🚫Neon underglow lights or strobe effects (hazard to other road users)
  • 🚫Removing catalytic converter or modifying exhaust to bypass BS6 emission norms
  • 🚫Changing seating capacity beyond RC-approved limits (e.g. adding seats to 4-seater)
  • 🚫Aftermarket LPG/CNG kits not certified by ARAI or PESO
  • 🚫Roof chopping, body widening or any structural alteration to the vehicle
⚠️ Note: Always get ARAI certification and update your RC at the RTO before driving with any structural modification. Our AutoIndia certified workshop handles all paperwork for legal mods. Contact us for guidance.
